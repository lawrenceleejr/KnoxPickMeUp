# Knox Pick-Me-Up — Pitch Emails

Ready-to-send first-contact emails for the pilot's institutional partners.
Each one leads with what *that* partner gets, makes a small specific ask, and
links the site and the briefing book. Fill the `[bracketed]` bits, send from
an address you watch (or `hello@knoxpickmeup.org`), and follow the sequence in
[PROGRAM.md §5](PROGRAM.md): **City + KPD first** — their credibility unlocks
everyone else — then parking and the Alliance, then KAT, with venue
recruitment running alongside.

Every email links:
- the site — <https://knoxpickmeup.org>
- the briefing book — <https://knoxpickmeup.org/design/Knox-Pick-Me-Up-Program-Details.pdf>

Keep the voice warm and wry, never preachy — the program thanks people for a
good call; it doesn't lecture. And keep the numbers honest: they're sourced in
[PROGRAM.md](PROGRAM.md) §1 and §4.

## 1. City of Knoxville

**To:** [Mayor's office / Office of Community Safety contact]
**Subject:** A positive-incentive complement to enforcement — 6-month downtown pilot, ready to go

Dear [Name],

In the Knoxville region, crashes involving an impaired driver seriously injure
about 67 people and kill 27 every year — and the single biggest reason people
talk themselves into driving home from downtown is the car they parked there.

**Knox Pick-Me-Up** flips the car from the problem into the guarantee. A patron
who shows their bartender a booked ride home at last call gets a serialized
card: a free large coffee at a downtown shop the next morning — when they come
back for the car — and a free KAT ride to get there. Knoxville saying *thanks
for keeping our roads safe*, capped at a coffee.

We're preparing a 6-month pilot: 3 bars, 3 coffee shops, serialized cards with
scan-based tracking, and quarterly issuance/redemption data by venue shared
with the City in full. The coffee is carried by the shops as customer
acquisition and the rides are KAT's in-kind contribution, so the City ask is
mostly *not* money:

1. Co-promote what's already true — downtown's public garages and lots are
   free evenings and weekends, so the car can wait until morning;
2. Include the launch in City communications, ideally tied to a high-visibility
   weekend;
3. Optionally, one-time seed toward fixed costs — the full 6-month pilot is
   roughly $18k all-in (about $2,000–3,500 of that is printing, quote in hand),
   and one prevented impaired-driving crash costs the city more than all of it.

The full program design — mechanics, fraud controls, funding model, and the
evidence base — is in our briefing book:
knoxpickmeup.org/design/Knox-Pick-Me-Up-Program-Details.pdf, with the program
at knoxpickmeup.org. Could we take 20 minutes to walk you through it?

With thanks,

## 2. Knoxville Police Department

**To:** [Chief's office / community engagement contact]
**Subject:** A carrot to pair with the stick — free coffee for a documented safe ride home

Dear [Name],

One in three Knox County crash deaths involves an impaired driver. Enforcement
carries the stick; we'd like to hand you a carrot to go with it.

**Knox Pick-Me-Up** rewards the safe choice at the exact moment it's made: a
patron shows their bartender a booked ride home at last call and gets a
serialized card — a free large coffee downtown the next morning, plus a free
KAT ride back to their parked car. Every card handed out is a documented
decision not to drive impaired.

What we ask of KPD is deliberately small:

1. A public endorsement at launch — your credibility is what makes bars,
   shops, and the City take a new program seriously;
2. A mention at the moments that matter: football Saturdays, holiday
   weekends, New Year's.

Just as important is what we *don't* ask: no enforcement role inside bars, no
data on individuals — cards are anonymous by design, and the program must
never feel like a checkpoint. You'd get quarterly issuance and redemption
data by venue, and we'd welcome your read on the downtown crash numbers as
the pilot runs.

The full design is at knoxpickmeup.org and in the briefing book:
knoxpickmeup.org/design/Knox-Pick-Me-Up-Program-Details.pdf. 20 minutes,
any format you like?

## 3. Downtown parking (PBA / City parking team)

**To:** [Downtown parking / garage operations contact]
**Subject:** Your free-evening parking is our best road-safety tool — one messaging ask

Dear [Name],

The single biggest reason people drive home impaired from downtown is the
parked car — "I can't just leave it." Except downtown, they can: public
garages and lots are already free evenings and weekends. **Knox Pick-Me-Up**
is a road-safety program built on advertising exactly that.

The mechanics in one line: a patron who shows their bartender a booked ride
home gets a card for a free coffee the next morning — when they come back for
the car — plus a free KAT ride to get there. The car stops being the reason to
drive and becomes the reason they return.

Our asks are about accuracy and reach, not money:

1. **Confirm the official language** on evening/weekend free parking and
   overnight stays — posted hours, any move-out times, event-pricing
   exceptions — so every coaster and sign we print states it exactly right;
2. **Co-promotion in the garages** — elevator and pay-station signage saying
   "left the car? good call" (we supply all artwork);
3. Your guidance on facilities where overnight parking *isn't* ideal, and
   introductions to private lot operators who might join as sponsors.

Program details: knoxpickmeup.org, full briefing book at
knoxpickmeup.org/design/Knox-Pick-Me-Up-Program-Details.pdf. Could we get 20
minutes to align the messaging?

## 5. Knoxville Area Transit (KAT)

**To:** [KAT planning / marketing contact]
**Subject:** "Hair of the KAT" — riders home safe tonight, back on your bus tomorrow

Dear [Name],

**Knox Pick-Me-Up** rewards downtown patrons who take a safe ride home at last
call with a card for a free coffee the next morning. We'd like KAT built in as
a full partner, two ways:

1. **An activated KAT ticket counts as ride proof.** Someone taking the bus
   home safely deserves the perk as much as anyone booking a rideshare —
   showing the bartender an activated ticket earns the card.
2. **A valid card doubles as a free KAT fare** during its one-day window —
   we call it *Hair of the KAT* — covering the morning trip back downtown to
   the parked car.

What KAT gets: your service in front of the exact crowd that needs a late
ride, at the exact moment they're deciding — with bartenders, coasters, and
signage across downtown pointing to the bus. First-time riders, off-peak
boardings, and a road-safety story worth telling. Exposure stays naturally
capped: each card is valid one day, and boardings can be tallied by driver
count or farebox code for clean, anonymous ridership data.

We're framing the rides as KAT's in-kind contribution to a shared road-safety
goal rather than a program payout — and we'd also love your help getting
late-night routes and hours in front of patrons and bar staff.

Full design: knoxpickmeup.org and
knoxpickmeup.org/design/Knox-Pick-Me-Up-Program-Details.pdf. Could we set up
20 minutes with your team?

## Other partners worth approaching

Beyond the five above, in rough order of value to the pilot:

| Partner | The angle |
|---|---|
| **Tennessee Highway Safety Office** | Grant money for exactly this category ([PROGRAM.md §4E](PROGRAM.md)); the pilot's metrics plan is already grant-shaped — application prep and timing in [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md) |
| **University of Tennessee / UT Athletics** | Football Saturdays are the peak-risk nights; gameday comms and campus channels reach the exact crowd (the card is already Vol orange) |
| **Knox County Health Department** | Already the program's data source; public-health credibility and help with honest evaluation |
| **UT Medical Center (trauma services)** | Hospitals fund injury-prevention outreach; a natural sponsor tier ([PROGRAM.md §4C](PROGRAM.md)) |
| **Uber / Lyft + local taxi companies** | The program drives bookings — the ride *is* the ticket; sponsorship, in-app promos |
| **Visit Knoxville** | Visiting fans and convention-goers don't know the parking is free or where the bus runs — "get home easy" for out-of-towners |
| **AAA East Tennessee & auto insurers** | Classic road-safety sponsors; fewer crashes is literally their business model |
| **Private parking operators** | Sponsor the "your car's fine overnight" message where a public garage isn't nearby |
| **A local print shop** | The "printing donated by" credit is already built into the card backs and coasters ([PRINTING.md](PRINTING.md)) — Graphic Creations is the live conversation |
| **Neighborhood & merchant orgs** (Old City, Market Square) | Door-openers for venue recruitment in the pilot footprint |
| **Local coffee roasters** | Subsidize partner shops' redemption cost in beans — on-brand and cheap goodwill |
| **Road-safety nonprofits** (MADD East TN, etc.) | Endorsements and volunteers — lead with the safe-ride frame; gauge fit, since some prefer abstinence-only messaging to harm reduction |

Two cautions from [PROGRAM.md](PROGRAM.md): keep **alcohol brands off
patron-facing materials** (a distributor can sponsor the program, not the
card), and keep KPD an **endorser, never an operator** — the moment the
program feels like surveillance, bars stop handing out cards.
